What can AI actually do in a business like mine?

+
Anything repetitive, rule-based or waiting on a human to read something. In practice that usually means admin and data entry, quoting and follow-ups, answering the same customer questions, and pulling reports. We start with an audit so you see the shortlist before spending anything.

How long does implementation take?

+
Weeks, not quarters. A focused automation is usually live inside two to four weeks; a broader system rolls out in stages so you get value from the first one while we build the next.

Do I need technical people on my side?

No. We build it, integrate it and train whoever will be using it. You keep full visibility, and with outreach you approve the prospect list and the messaging before anything is sent.

Privacy Policy

Treadsoft ("we", "us") provides AI implementation, automation and outbound outreach services to businesses. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. It covers this website and the services we deliver to our clients.

We are the data controller for the information described in this policy unless stated otherwise. You can reach us at info@treadsoft.co or 1623 Warwick Rd, Knowle, Solihull, B93 9LF, United Kingdom.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you complete a form on this site or book a consultation, we collect your name, work email address, company name and anything you write in the message field.
  • Correspondence. Emails, calls and meeting notes relating to your enquiry or engagement.
  • Technical information. Basic data your browser sends when you visit: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and referring site, used to keep the site secure and understand how it is used.
  • Client project data. Where we build or run systems for a client, we may process personal data held in their tools (for example CRM records or support enquiries) on their instructions.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services: necessary to perform a contract with you, or to take steps at your request before entering one.
  • To send relevant business-to-business communications: our legitimate interests in promoting our services, balanced against your rights. You can opt out at any time.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website: our legitimate interests in running a safe, functional site.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations: compliance with a legal obligation.

Outbound outreach

Our outreach service contacts business contacts on behalf of our clients. We use business contact details obtained from public sources and reputable data providers, and we rely on legitimate interests for business-to-business contact. Every message identifies who is contacting you and how to opt out; if you ask to be removed, we suppress your details and do not contact you again. For these campaigns our client is the data controller and we act as their processor.

Artificial intelligence

Our services use AI systems to draft messages, classify enquiries and automate tasks. We use business-grade AI providers under terms that prevent your data being used to train their public models, we limit the data sent to what a task requires, and a person remains accountable for decisions that affect you. We do not use AI to make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only where necessary: with service providers who host our website, deliver email, run our CRM and provide AI processing; with our professional advisers; with a client where the data relates to their campaign or system; and with authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data. All providers act under contract and may only use the data to deliver their service to us.

International transfers

Some of our providers are based outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred abroad we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement (or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum), together with additional safeguards where needed.

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. Client records are kept for the duration of the engagement and then for as long as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes. Suppression lists are kept indefinitely so we can honour opt-outs. Data we process for a client is retained or deleted on their instructions.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies and local storage needed for it to function, plus, where enabled, privacy-conscious analytics to count visits. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, least-privilege permissions and vetted providers to protect personal data. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information and will notify you and the regulator of a qualifying breach as required.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your data, correct it, ask us to delete it, restrict or object to our processing, request portability, and withdraw consent where we relied on it. To exercise any of these, email info@treadsoft.co and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The current version always appears on this page, and the date below tells you when it was last revised.
